First 100 everyday Mandarin words

Twenty lessons of 4–6 entries. Prepared 1 November 2026 for Hanzi Hunters.

Dictionary-checked readings and selected meanings. Notes, examples, and teaching sequence are AI-assisted editorial drafts, not human-teacher-approved. Official mappings refer to the HSK syllabus published 2025-11, effective 2026-07. 98 entries map to level 1; 咖啡 and 累 map to level 2.

Polite everyday expressions

#WordPinyinMeaningTeaching noteOfficial reference
1你好nǐ hǎohelloA common greeting. Both syllables are written with third tones; in ordinary speech the first changes to a rising tone: ní hǎo.Level 1; entry 147; printed p. 80
2谢谢xièxiethank youUse to thank someone. The second syllable is neutral and usually shorter and lighter than the first.Level 1; entry 241; printed p. 83
3再见zàijiàngoodbyeA common way to say goodbye. Both syllables have fourth tones.Level 1; entry 271; printed p. 83
4请qǐngplease / inviteBefore an action, 请 makes a polite request. 请坐 (qǐng zuò) means please sit down.Level 1; entry 165; printed p. 81
5对不起duìbuqǐsorry / excuse meUse to apologize or politely get someone's attention. In this expression, bu is neutral; it is not the ordinary isolated reading bù.Level 1; entry 45; printed p. 78

People in a conversation

#WordPinyinMeaningTeaching noteOfficial reference
6我wǒI / meUse 我 for yourself. Chinese pronouns do not change form between I and me.Level 1; entry 221; printed p. 82
7你nǐyouThe usual singular you. 您 (nín) is a more respectful form.Level 1; entry 146; printed p. 80
8他tāhe / himWritten 他 refers to he or him. It sounds exactly like 她 (she/her), so speech needs context.Level 1; entry 200; printed p. 81
9她tāshe / herWritten 她 refers to she or her. It sounds exactly like 他 (he/him); the 女 component helps distinguish the writing.Level 1; entry 204; printed p. 82
10您nínyou (polite)A respectful singular form of you, often used with elders, customers, or someone you wish to address politely.Level 1; entry 150; printed p. 80

Your first descriptions and questions

#WordPinyinMeaningTeaching noteOfficial reference
11是shìbe (identity)Links a person or thing to an identity or category: 我是学生 means I am a student. It is not a general equivalent of English yes or the is before an adjective.Level 1; entry 188; printed p. 81
12人rénperson / peopleCan refer to a person or to people; the character itself does not mark singular or plural. 三个人 means three people.Level 1; entry 170; printed p. 81
13好hǎogoodUse hǎo for good or well. 好 also has the reading hào in other words, so its pronunciation depends on meaning.Level 1; entry 71; printed p. 78
14很hěnveryOften comes before an adjective. In an ordinary description it may simply make the sentence natural, without strongly emphasizing very.Level 1; entry 79; printed p. 78
15吗mamarks a yes/no questionAt the end of a statement, 吗 makes a yes/no question. This particle is neutral tone; do not add it automatically to questions already containing 什么 or 谁.Level 1; entry 115; printed p. 79

Names and friends

#WordPinyinMeaningTeaching noteOfficial reference
16叫jiàobe called / callUse 我叫... to give your name. 叫 also means to call or shout in other contexts.Level 1; entry 91; printed p. 79
17名字míngzinameMeans a person's or thing's name. The second syllable, zi, is neutral.Level 1; entry 128; printed p. 80
18谁shéi / shuíwhoAsks which person is meant. Shéi and shuí are both accepted pronunciations of this word.Level 1; entry 181; printed p. 81
19什么shénmewhatUse to ask what something is or what someone does or wants. Me is neutral; 什么 stays where the answer would go in the sentence.Level 1; entry 182; printed p. 81
20朋友péngyoufriendAn everyday word for friend. You is neutral in the pronunciation taught here.Level 1; entry 156; printed p. 80

Having, negating, and belonging

#WordPinyinMeaningTeaching noteOfficial reference
21不bùnotUsually negates present or habitual actions and descriptions. Before a fourth-tone syllable it is normally pronounced bú. Negate 有 with 没有 instead.Level 1; entry 13; printed p. 77
22也yěalso / tooUsually comes before the verb or adjective phrase. 我也去 means I am going too.Level 1; entry 253; printed p. 83
23有yǒuhave / there isExpresses possession or existence. 我有书 means I have a book or books. Its usual negative is 没有, not 不有.Level 1; entry 263; printed p. 83
24没有méiyǒunot have / there is noNegates 有: 我没有书 means I do not have a book. It can also negate an action that has not happened; start with possession here.Level 1; entry 122; printed p. 80
25的demarks possession / descriptionLinks an owner or description to a noun: 我的书 means my book. This grammatical use is neutral de; the character has other readings in other words.Level 1; entry 30; printed p. 77

Pointing and choosing

#WordPinyinMeaningTeaching noteOfficial reference
26这zhè / zhèithisPoints to something nearby or being discussed. Compare 那, that. Before classifiers you may also hear zhèi.Level 1; entry 279; printed p. 83
27那nà / nèithatPoints to something farther away or already mentioned. Compare 这, this. Before classifiers you may also hear nèi.Level 1; entry 136; printed p. 80
28哪nǎ / něiwhichAsks which one. 哪个 (nǎ ge) means which one; 哪 is different from 那 (nà, that).Level 1; entry 131; printed p. 80
29这里zhèlǐhereRefers to this place. 我在这里 means I am here. 这儿 is another common form, not included as a separate entry in this course.Level 1; entry 282; printed p. 84
30个gègeneral classifierA common counting word between a number and a noun: 三个人 means three people. Its dictionary tone is fourth; it is often neutral in phrases such as 一个 (yí ge).Level 1; entry 60; printed p. 78

Numbers one to five

#WordPinyinMeaningTeaching noteOfficial reference
31一yī / yāooneYī is the dictionary reading and is used in counting. Before a fourth tone it normally becomes yí; before a first, second, or third tone it normally becomes yì.Level 1; entry 254; printed p. 83
32二èrtwo (counting)Use 二 when counting or reading digits. Before most classifiers, 两 is normally used instead: 两个人 means two people.Level 1; entry 49; printed p. 78
33三sānthreeFirst tone: high and level. 三个人 means three people.Level 1; entry 173; printed p. 81
34四sìfourFourth tone: a clear fall. 四 (sì) and 十 (shí, ten) have different initials and tones.Level 1; entry 198; printed p. 81
35五wǔfiveThird tone. In ordinary speech a third tone is often low rather than a full dip and rise.Level 1; entry 223; printed p. 82

Counting, quantity, and size

#WordPinyinMeaningTeaching noteOfficial reference
41零língzeroUse 零 for zero, including when reading digits in a phone number. It has a second tone.Level 1; entry 112; printed p. 79
42两liǎngtwo (before classifiers)Normally used before classifiers: 两个人 means two people. Use 二 for counting one, two, three and for most digit-by-digit numbers.Level 1; entry 111; printed p. 79
43多duōmany / muchDescribes a large quantity. 很多 means many or a lot; compare 少, few or little.Level 1; entry 46; printed p. 78
44少shǎofew / littleDescribes a small quantity. This meaning is shǎo; shào belongs to other uses of the character.Level 1; entry 180; printed p. 81
45大dàbig / largeDescribes something big or large. In a simple description say 很大, rather than inserting 是 before 大.Level 1; entry 25; printed p. 77
46小xiǎosmallDescribes something small. Compare 大, big. 小 can also relate to being young in other contexts.Level 1; entry 234; printed p. 82

Home and family

#WordPinyinMeaningTeaching noteOfficial reference
47家jiāhome / familyCan mean home or family. 回家 means return home; 家 does not only refer to the physical building.Level 1; entry 86; printed p. 79
48妈妈māmamom / motherAn everyday word for mother. The first syllable has first tone and the second is neutral.Level 1; entry 114; printed p. 79
49爸爸bàbadad / fatherAn everyday word for father. The first syllable has fourth tone and the second is neutral.Level 1; entry 3; printed p. 77

51喜欢xǐhuanlike / be fond ofCan be followed by a noun or an activity: 喜欢茶 means like tea; 喜欢看书 means like reading. Huan is neutral here.Level 1; entry 225; printed p. 82

Wants, eating, and drinking

#WordPinyinMeaningTeaching noteOfficial reference
57想xiǎngthink / want toBefore an action, 想 can express wanting to do it. 想喝茶 means want to drink tea. It can also mean think or miss someone.Level 1; entry 233; printed p. 82
58要yàowant / needExpresses wanting or needing something. 我要茶 means I want tea; 想 often sounds softer when expressing a wish to do something.Level 1; entry 252; printed p. 83
59吃chīeatUse for eating. 吃饭 (chīfàn) means to eat or have a meal; it does not require the meal to contain rice.Level 1; entry 21; printed p. 77
60喝hēdrinkUse with drinks: 喝水 means drink water. The reading taught here is hē; 喝 has another reading in other uses.Level 1; entry 77; printed p. 78
61水shuǐwater喝水 means drink water. The final ui is written with the tone mark on i: shuǐ.Level 1; entry 192; printed p. 81

Today, tomorrow, and when
